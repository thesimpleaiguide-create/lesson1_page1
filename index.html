Conflict with patients is one of the most draining parts of working in healthcare and one of the least talked about. This lesson will give you practical tools to handle those moments with confidence, without you paying an emotional cost.

We will start with the why behind difficult behaviour because understanding what is driving it changes everything. Then we will move to the how, that is, what you can actually do in the moment.

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Recognise the hidden drivers behind challenging behaviour so that you can respond rather than react
  • Use empathy-first communication to de-escalate tense situations without losing your authority
  • Apply the 'what is in my control' model to focus your energy on what you can influence which will reduce stress and achieve better outcomes for you and the patient.
